How to Use It

  1. For a normal email, fill in the form and tap Open in Apple Mail.
  2. For audio, choose the MP3/WAV files and tap Share Audio to Mail.
  3. When the Apple share sheet appears, choose Mail.
  4. If Mail does not carry over the recipient, tap the To field and paste. This app copies it for you first.

Important: iPhone/iPad browsers do not have permission to inject local files into an already-open Mail draft. That is an Apple/browser limitation. The share sheet is the reliable browser method for sending the actual audio files to Mail.

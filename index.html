How it works

Three taps. Any device.

Works the same on Mac, Windows, Android, iPhone, Linux — anywhere there's a default mail app.

01
Sign in & write
Sign in with Google (one-time), then fill recipient, subject, body, and drop in any audio files.
02
Send with Gmail
Tap Send. The email goes out from your Gmail account with the styled body and attached audio. Saves to your Sent folder.
03
Offline fallback
No internet? Use Copy styled email or .eml — the whole builder still works. Bring it online later to send.
